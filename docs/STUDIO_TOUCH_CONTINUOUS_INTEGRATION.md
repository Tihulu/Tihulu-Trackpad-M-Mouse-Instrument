# Touch continuous-control integration
Connect multichannel touch frames and compatibility single-touch bend to the existing priority ledger. Fretless winning bend/range is established before a new note-on; frame CC1 and CC11 use the final value per channel, preserving prior last-contact semantics without duplicate emission. Retired MPE channels keep bend and CC until final pedal release, then hand off to a remaining owner or neutral. Touch release and bridge stop are scoped to their owners. Two combined ledger/pool regressions authored. Channel pressure remains on the previous direct path; full MPE zone setup, held-contact fretted changes, and DAW receiver delivery remain unverified. Fixed priority is a design choice; lower-priority controls retain their latest value while masked. Native integration and actual MIDI monitor acceptance pending. Stable unchanged.
