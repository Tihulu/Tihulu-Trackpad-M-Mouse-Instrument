# R9 touch edge remediation

Frozen PERF reproduction: short touch down/up while piano frame awaits ACK sent only release, losing onset. This patch queues up to eight ordered XY onset/release edges with original onset coordinates. Continuous motion remains coalesced; no refresh ahead of queued edges. A release at the queue head is prioritized but cannot overtake its onset. Each edge must be sent before 150 ms of age, or connection closes, preserving existing watchdog and ACK limits. Pointer cancellation/lost capture while held closes and discards pending edges; normal pointer-up and subsequent lost capture do not cancel completed taps. Rotation with active/pending touch closes. No claim of delivery during overload, scheduler fairness or native/physical acceptance. No capacity change. Five new VM scenarios; independent PERF re-review required.
