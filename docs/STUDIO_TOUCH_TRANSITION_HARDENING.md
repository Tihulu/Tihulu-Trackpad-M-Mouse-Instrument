# Legacy touch note transition hardening
A held MPE contact crossing a fretted note under sustain retires the old member and allocates a new free member, never retuning the old sustained voice. A mode, instrument/channel or fretless change also releases the old routing first. Initial CC/pressure and fretless pitch are established before new note-on. Ignore duplicate contact IDs and invalid numeric samples; reject invalid pitch spans before conversion. Two pure transition regressions authored; service ordering and real MIDI acceptance remain pending. Existing conservative MPE exhaustion behavior remains. No stable changes.
