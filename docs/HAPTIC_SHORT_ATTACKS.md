# Short physical haptic attacks

Candidate base: #143 head efe398165062fa8ea05ca042c85bfaee03870b6b. #142 and #143 inherited a test expectation that conflicted with intentional retention of an unchanged mapping ID/calibration; that expectation was repaired in both branches before this candidate.

The native Touch Instrument, standalone live-note renderer and incoming MIDI haptic renderer previously sampled held notes on timers; a complete down/up between ticks could vanish. A shared bounded Swift HapticAttackBuffer records recent attack intents, at most four batches, with maximum age 40 ms. Overflow keeps recent attacks; expired or invalid timestamps are discarded. This is a physical best-effort pulse policy, not a MIDI event queue. No MIDI gate, controller ownership, remote deadline, hardware tone count, or timer cadence is changed.

Touch melodic/fretted/fretless/string/chord onsets retain short recent contact groups; currently held contacts take precedence. Existing drum-hit path and drum priority remain. The incoming MIDI bridge merges recent attack commands into available motor voices, retaining existing held-note priority. Standalone and optional shared note output accept explicit note attacks from Chord Pads, progression, Touch Bar and gamepad commands; release-all, mute and session retirement clear pending attacks. The shared renderer submits transient pulses only for one tick and clears their mixer intent afterward. No new hardware writer is introduced.

Exact core blob bd5c8d693bdd4e2e247787b00670d7a637573ff6 was reconstructed and hash-verified in Linux, compiled with Swift 6.2.1 and passed 20,006 assertions over 10,000 isolated short attacks plus expiry, capacity, grouping, invalid clock and clear cases. Three core regression tests and one macOS injected-sink test authored. Full CI and physical performance acceptance pending.

Limitations: four recent batches, six physical tones, timer and motor limitations mean simultaneous or repeated ultra-short attacks can still be merged, displaced or expire. Existing raw contact and GameController callback delivery limitations remain. Haptic pulses do not guarantee audible DAW audio timing. Physical test required for trackpad and Magic Mouse fast taps, fretted slides and fretless MPE, drums, chord pads, Touch Bar, gamepads and incoming MIDI, including overlapping sources and explicit release/stop. Other paths (camera/mobile Builder haptics, sequencer, audio/voice, shared broader output) require their own acceptance; stable installation unchanged.
