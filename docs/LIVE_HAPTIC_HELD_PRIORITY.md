Previously LiveNoteHapticOutput and SharedLiveNoteOutput selected the oldest buffered attack frame instead of the current held frame whenever a pending attack existed. This could temporarily replace the current pitch/chord. Both now use LiveHapticFrameSelection. With valid simultaneous frames it retains the current tone order and appends distinct transient frequencies up to the existing six-tone cap. With no extra capacity or no distinct extra pitch it returns the current frame unchanged; with no held frame it retains the original attack-only behavior. Mixed duration is the shorter input duration, and maximum level is the larger original input level, not summed. Shared tone energy can change when mixing. Invalid buffered attacks do not replace a valid held frame. No new timer, queue, safety deadline, driver ownership, physical device or actuator capacity. The existing shared coordinator can still allocate its six tones across the two active sources; this policy is per-source and does not guarantee every held voice across multiple sources. Attack snapshots contain whole frames rather than per-note identity; this is best-effort, not lossless reconstruction. Full six-tone held frames intentionally take precedence over distinct pending attacks, so some short attacks may be inaudible. No physical motor or audio latency claim.
