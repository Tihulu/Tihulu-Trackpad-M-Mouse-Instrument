# Quality gate Q2a — scoped source retirement

First increment of shared output reliability, not a mixer. Preserve legacy rendering, tone limits, durations and normal MIDI endpoint persistence. Touch Instrument and incoming MIDI render timers each capture an independent revocable lease. Stop revokes that lease synchronously before returning; a later restart cannot validate an old timer. Touch drum snapshots are taken under the current lease and drum priority is reset for a new lease. Disabling incoming MIDI haptics drains in-flight submissions and rechecks enabled state immediately before hardware submission. Q1's terminal driver fence still protects Panic and Reconnect.

The service lifecycle methods remain serialized by the existing controller; this does not claim arbitrary concurrent starts/stops are supported. Already submitted physical frames can finish their bounded duration. No assertion of zero physical vibration at the instant of Stop. No new UI or instrument features. The optional live-note and speaker engines retain their existing separate serialized stop mechanisms.

Tests cover independent source leases, revocation/drain and old-token rejection with mock submissions. Real Mac acceptance: rapidly stop/restart touch and incoming MIDI with notes held; disable input haptics while playing; verify no stale drum hits or notes, independent MIDI output remains active, no change to current sound, and Panic/Reconnect regression. Next: one shared frame arbiter and source-aware mixing, after exact-head CI and native testing. Do not claim shared simultaneous haptic mixing is implemented.
