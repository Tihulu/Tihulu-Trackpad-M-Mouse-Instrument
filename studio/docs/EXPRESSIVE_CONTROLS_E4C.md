# Expressive Controls E4c — camera choice and lifecycle hardening

Local session-only device selection (explicit refresh, default camera or enumerated device), manual normalized X/Y rotation and horizontal mirror; no automatic hardware fallback when selected camera disappears. Rotation is applied to derived controls, not raw frames. Settings editable while stopped, no device IDs in exported instruments. Two fresh reliable frames required to acquire; loss is immediate; stale/nonmonotonic acquisition is rejected and a gap resets acquisition. A stale queued frame stops the Builder rather than replaying it. Permission loss, capture runtime error, selected device disconnect or lack of fresh frame delivery (8s startup, 2s subsequent) stops the Builder, preserving the earlier 350ms MIDI ownership watchdog. Previous camera teardown must finish before another camera session starts. Camera observers removed on teardown. No additional video analysis, recording, upload, idle polling, automatic resume or new dependency. Three deterministic tests for transforms, reacquisition and real router note release. Native physical permission/disconnect, camera list coverage, focus/sleep, orientation and latency measurements still required. Stable untouched.
